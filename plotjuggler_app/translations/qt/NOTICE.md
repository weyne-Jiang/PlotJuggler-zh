# Upstream Qt Simplified Chinese catalogue

`qt_zh_CN.ts` is an unmodified copy of the community Chinese Qt catalogue
from `qt/qttranslations`, tag `v5.15.2`, commit
`cb1857418b36780b0444333f3aa6250ca3780f19`.

Source: https://github.com/qt/qttranslations/blob/v5.15.2/translations/qt_zh_CN.ts

SHA256: `31A96ABF5B37057D0802C3A6CB0000820EBA0AFFEFD1EA1A655E95E1B5E1F0A5`

This older Chinese catalogue is monolithic, including Qt standard controls.
It is compiled to `qt_zh_CN.qm` and embedded with the application translation.
Upstream source and notices are included in deployments for attribution and
maintenance. See `UPSTREAM-README` and upstream `LICENSE.GPL3-EXCEPT`.
Qt binary translation files are redistributable under the Qt translation
licensing terms; the Qt runtime's own license material is also packaged.
